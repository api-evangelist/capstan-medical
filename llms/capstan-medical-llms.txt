# Capstan Medical

> Capstan Medical is a Santa Cruz, California medical device company founded in 2020 out of the Occam Labs incubator. It builds a surgical robotics platform for structural heart disease, combining robotic catheter navigation with next-generation transcatheter mitral and tricuspid valve implants so heart valves can be repaired or replaced without open heart surgery. Capstan is pre-commercial and publishes no public API, developer portal, SDKs, or machine-readable API contracts.

## APIs

Capstan Medical publishes no public API. Contract discovery on 2026-08-01 found no
OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL, AsyncAPI, MCP, or A2A surface on capstanmedical.com, and no
api/developer/docs/portal subdomain resolves. This is expected for a pre-commercial,
FDA-regulated implantable device and capital-equipment company: the robot and the
implants are the product, not a developer platform.
